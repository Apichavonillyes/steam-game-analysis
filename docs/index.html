        KEY FINDINGS

        ✔

        PRICE VS. REVIEWS

        Games priced $10–19 had the best reviews, at 88% positive on average. Free games had the worst, at 77%. Above $20, reviews slowly got worse. Players seem to expect more from pricier games.

        ✔

        GENRES

        Indie games got the best reviews (87%). Massively multiplayer games got the worst (72%) but had the most owners, about 5.9 million on average. A game can be very popular and still get mixed reviews.

        ✔

        GROWTH

        This data can't answer it well. Every genre looks like it drops after 2020, but newer games just haven't had time to build up owners. I would need data on every game released each year.

        ✔

        LAUNCH MONTH

        Averages made February, November and August look best. But the typical game had 1–2 million owners in every month. A few huge hits, like Counter-Strike and Apex Legends, pushed the averages up.

        !

        ADVICE FOR A SMALL STUDIO

        Price the game around $10–19, and don't count on launch timing to make it a hit.
